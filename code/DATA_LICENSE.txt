The real-matrix study uses the PARSEC/Si2, PARSEC/SiH4, PARSEC/benzene,
and PARSEC/Si5H12 matrices from the SuiteSparse Matrix Collection:

https://sparse.tamu.edu

The SuiteSparse Matrix Collection states that its matrices are licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The matrices are
not redistributed in this repository. The software downloads the original
Matrix Market archives, verifies pinned SHA-256 digests, and records derived
benchmark quantities. The matrices are attributed to Y. Zhou, Y. Saad,
M. Tiago, and J. Chelikowsky, with T. Davis as collection editor.
